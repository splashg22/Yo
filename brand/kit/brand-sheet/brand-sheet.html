Logo

The markA capital D sliced into three bands, 40 / 20 / 40 of its height: pink for the DCTY burn, violet for the buyback, cyan for holders. The bands are the rule; never change their order, colours or proportions. Clear spaceKeep a margin of half the mark's height on every side free of other elements (dashed box). Minimum size24 px on screen, 8 mm in print for the mark; 120 px / 30 mm for the lockup. The gaps still read at 16 px (favicon). Wordmark"Dacity" in DM Sans 800, tracking −4%; "STRATEGY" in DM Sans 500, tracking +34%, in cyan on dark and deep pink on light.

Do and don't

Do use the mark on plum, on the wall texture, or on pale pink.
Don't recolour, flatten to one colour, or swap the band colours.
Don't rotate, stretch or skew.
Do allow one soft pink drop-shadow on dark; never a halo on light.
Don't place it on a busy photo without a dark scrim.
Do use the lockup above 120 px; below that use the mark alone.

Voice

Short sentences. One rule, said plainly: 40% burns DCTY, 20% buys the coin back, 40% is paid to holders in TTWO. Never a figure that is not on the chain. No badges, stamps or "LIVE" readouts. Independent project, not affiliated with Take-Two Interactive, the TTWO issuer or pump.fun.
