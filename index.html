Five steps, no hands on the money.

A small open-source bot runs the treasury on a fixed rule. It cannot change the split and it keeps nothing. Every step is a public Solana transaction.

Step 01 · Claim
Creator fees in TTWO

pump.fun pays the coin's creator a fee on every trade. Because the coin is paired with TTWO, the fee arrives in TTWO and waits in a vault only the treasury can claim. The bot claims it each cycle.

Step 02 · Split
40 / 20 / 40

Every claim is divided three ways by a fixed rule: 40% to burn DCTY, 20% to buy this coin back, 40% to holders. Nothing is kept and no one can change the ratio without changing the public code.

Step 03 · Burn
Buy DCTY, destroy it

The first share buys DacityCoin on the open market and burns it in the same cycle. Supply only goes down, and each burn is a transaction anyone can open.

Step 04 · Buy back
Buy this coin back, destroy it

The second share buys Dacity Strategy on its own market and burns it too. The coin you hold gets scarcer with every cycle, and every buyback is on-chain.

Step 05 · Pay
Holders are paid in TTWO

The last share is sent, pro rata, to every wallet holding at least the minimum. Nothing to claim: it simply lands in your wallet.
