How it works

STEP 1
Fees come inEvery trade of the strategy coin on pump.fun pays a creator fee in TTWO (tokenized Take-Two stock) to the treasury wallet.
STEP 2
Split in halfEach claim is split 50 / 50. Half buys DCTY, half is set aside for holders. Nothing is kept.
STEP 3
Buy and burn DCTYThe first half buys DacityCoin on the open market and burns it, permanently removing it from supply.
STEP 4
Pay holders in TTWOThe second half is sent in TTWO to every wallet holding at least … of the strategy coin, in proportion to its balance. No claiming needed.
