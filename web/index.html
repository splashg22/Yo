    Connecting…

    Four steps, no hands on the money

    A small open-source bot runs the treasury on a fixed rule. It cannot change the split and it keeps nothing. Every step is a public Solana transaction.

    1. Step 1 · Claim

      Creator fees in TTWO

      pump.fun pays the coin's creator a fee on every trade. Because the coin is paired with TTWO, the fee arrives in TTWO and waits in a vault only the treasury can claim. The bot claims it each cycle.

    2. Step 2 · Split

      50 / 50

      Every claim is divided exactly in half. Nothing is kept, and no one can change the ratio without changing the public code.

    3. Step 3 · Burn

      Buy DCTY, destroy it

      One half buys DacityCoin on the open market and burns it in the same cycle. Supply only goes down, and each burn is a transaction anyone can open.

    4. Step 4 · Pay

      Holders are paid in TTWO

      The other half is sent, pro rata, to every wallet holding at least the minimum. Nothing to claim: it simply lands in your wallet.
