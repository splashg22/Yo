How it works

A small open-source bot runs the treasury on a fixed rule. It cannot change the split and it keeps nothing.

  1. Creator fees are claimed in TTWO

    pump.fun pays the coin's creator a fee on every trade. Because the coin is paired with TTWO, the fee arrives in TTWO and waits in a vault only the treasury can claim. The bot claims it each cycle.

  2. Every claim is split 50 / 50

    Each claim is divided exactly in half. Nothing is kept, and no one can change the ratio without changing the public code.

  3. One half buys DCTY and burns it

    The bot buys DacityCoin on the open market and burns it in the same cycle. Supply only goes down, and each burn is a transaction anyone can open.

  4. The other half is paid to holders

    It is sent in TTWO, pro rata, to every wallet holding at least the minimum. Nothing to claim: it simply lands in your wallet.
