Questions

Do I have to claim anything?

No. If your wallet holds at least the minimum when a cycle runs, TTWO is sent to you. It arrives in a TTWO token account in your wallet, created for you if needed.

Why should I trust this page?

You do not have to. It is a static page with no server: your own browser asks a public Solana RPC for the treasury's history and shows every transaction with a link to an explorer. The bot's source code is public and the split is a fixed rule in it.

What is TTWO?

A token that tracks Take-Two Interactive stock, issued by a third party on Solana. The coin is traded against it on pump.fun, so fees and payouts are in TTWO. Issuer terms apply to TTWO itself. Nothing here is financial advice.

What if the bot is offline?

Fees keep accruing in pump.fun's creator vault, which only the treasury can claim. Nothing is lost. The next cycle claims everything that built up and splits it as usual.

Verify it yourself: open the treasury on any explorer. Its history is this page. Data comes from a public Solana RPC and can lag by a few seconds. Not financial advice.
